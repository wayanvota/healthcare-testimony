About the product

A public-record alignment engine for healthcare hearings.

The tool helps policy and communications teams prepare healthcare CEOs for Senate testimony without inventing senator positions, overstating alignment, or hiding evidence gaps.

Use it when the stakes are public.

The workflow is designed for political strategists, public affairs teams, congressional affairs staff, and CEO communications advisors.

Launch the tool

What it does

It turns testimony into committee prep.

Users enter a hearing title, committee, healthcare topic, company type, CEO name, organization, and draft testimony. The application extracts claims, maps them to healthcare issue tags, retrieves evidence fixtures or configured public sources, scores alignment, and generates red-team questions and safer rewrites.

Evidence discipline

Every senator-specific claim needs a source.

  • Direct evidence is separated from reasonable inference.
  • Thin evidence is labeled instead of filled in with speculation.
  • Recommendations are marked as strategic communications guidance, not facts.
  • Unsupported LLM-style senator claims are blocked by citation auditing.

Healthcare topics

Configured for issues senators actually press.

The taxonomy includes Medicare Advantage, prior authorization, AI in healthcare, drug pricing, PBMs, hospital consolidation, cybersecurity, NIH funding, rural health, long-term care, patient access, safety, quality, reimbursement, privacy, fraud, billing transparency, and price transparency.

Guardrails

Designed to avoid misleading testimony.

The tool does not infer private beliefs, fabricate quotes, promise future votes, imply senator support without evidence, recommend deception, or provide legal lobbying compliance advice.
